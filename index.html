Change data-widget to countdown, next, rates, differential or calculator. Options for the bank, theme, colour and more are in the documentation. On WordPress, use the plugin or a Custom HTML block.

The data covers the Federal Reserve, ECB, Bank of England, Bank of Japan, Bank of Canada, RBA, RBNZ, SNB, Norges Bank and Riksbank, and updates within minutes of each decision. It is also free as a dataset and through clients for JavaScript and Python.
